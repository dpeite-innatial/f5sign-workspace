# Slice 07 — One receipt row per (envelope, recipient), tenant-isolated, claimed once

| Tier | critical |
| Covers | AC-13 (RLS), AC-10b (the claim, on two connections) |
| Depends on | slice 02 (`ReceiptBoundaryFact`) |

## Goal
`evidence_audit.issued_receipt` (migration, RLS enabled + forced, tenant policy, tenant-stamp trigger, grants),
the `IssuedReceipt` aggregate with its derived `IssuedReceiptId`, the `IssuedReceiptRepository` port and its DBAL
adapter, and the test fakes/cleanup the next slices need.

## Why
"One row per (envelope, recipient), id derived from both, RLS like `issued_trail`" (§3 #4); ADR-0086 §2.4:
"claim with `INSERT … ON CONFLICT (id) DO NOTHING` inside `CustodyStore::writeAlongside()`, so the claim commits
with the bytes and a lost claim withdraws its custody."

## The table (decided here)
`id UUID PK, tenant_id UUID, envelope_id UUID, recipient_id UUID, custody_id UUID, boundary_type TEXT,
boundary_position BIGINT, issued_at TIMESTAMPTZ, conserved_until TIMESTAMPTZ, conserved_at TIMESTAMPTZ` (all NOT NULL
but the two conserve columns); constraints `issued_receipt_pkey`, `issued_receipt_recipient_uniq UNIQUE (envelope_id,
recipient_id)`, `issued_receipt_custody_id_uniq UNIQUE (custody_id)`, `issued_receipt_conserved_pair_chk`; `ENABLE` +
`FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`; policy `issued_receipt_tenant_isolation` (USING + WITH CHECK `tenant_id =
public.current_tenant_id()`); trigger `issued_receipt_set_tenant_id BEFORE INSERT … public.set_tenant_id_from_scope()`;
`GRANT SELECT, INSERT` and `GRANT UPDATE (conserved_until, conserved_at)` to `f5sign_app`. No DEFAULT, no FK.
`down()` revokes and drops the table. (The custodian's policy and funnel are slice 12's.)

## Prose to write
- `IssuedReceipt`: "The record that one recipient's receipt was issued: which custody holds the sealed PDF, and which
  fact of the log the receipt ends at (ADR-0086 §2.4). A recipient has at most one per envelope: the id is derived
  from the pair, and the registry's primary key refuses a second row. It records no fact. After issuance its one
  transition is the custodian's conservation, once, and only after its envelope has ended (ADR-0086 §2.5)."
- `IssuedReceiptId`: "Derived from the envelope and the recipient, never minted at random: `v5(NAMESPACE, envelope
  ':' recipient)`, both lowercased. A redelivered fact dispatches a command with the same subject. A change of the
  namespace gives every recipient a second receipt."
- Migration: "Creates `evidence_audit.issued_receipt`, the registry of issued receipts, one row per envelope and
  recipient (ADR-0086 §2.4). Shaped as `issued_trail` (`Version20260925150020`, amended by `Version20260928090940`),
  whose docblocks state the claim, the security and the Never list; they hold here with the pair `(envelope_id,
  recipient_id)` in place of the envelope."
- `DbalIssuedReceiptRepository`: as `DbalIssuedTrailRepository`'s, with "receipt" for "trail".

## Claims you may NOT copy
- `IssuedTrail`'s "An envelope has at most one" — here one per recipient of an envelope.
