# Slice 09 — Three reactors issue a recipient's receipt on the fact that ends their act

| Tier | critical |
| Covers | AC-1 (trigger), AC-2 (trigger), AC-4b (the decline reactor), AC-14f–AC-14h |
| Depends on | slice 08 |

## Goal
`OnRecipientSigned`, `OnSessionCompleted` and `OnSessionDeclined` in `EvidenceAudit/Application/Subscriber`, each
paired with a Messenger handler, dispatch `IssueReceipt` as a pure function of the fact (envelope, recipient, the
fact's type and `subject()`, a fixed issuer, the fact's instant). A census ties every recipient outcome and session
status to the fact that does — or does not — bring a receipt.

## Why
"The trigger is the fact that makes the receipt true (owner). Under `SEQUENTIAL_PADES` that is `RECIPIENT_SIGNED`,
not the commit, for an approver too" (§4 #1). "The boundary is the triggering fact: its type and its subject." (§3 #2)

## The mapping (decided here)
`IssueReceipt(envelopeId: EnvelopeId::fromString(<event envelope>->toString()), recipientId:
RecipientId::fromString(<event recipient>->toString()), fact: ReceiptBoundaryFact::<X>, subjectId:
$event->subject()->toString(), issuedBy: SystemActorId::eventSubscriber('evidence_audit.on_recipient_signed' |
'evidence_audit.on_session_completed' | 'evidence_audit.on_session_declined'), issuedAt: $event->occurredAt)`.
`RecipientSigned` carries `envelopeId`/`recipientId` (`Target…Ref`); `SessionCompleted`/`SessionDeclined` carry
`envelopeRef`/`recipientRef`.

## Census (decided here, `ReceiptTriggersCensusTest`)
- `every_receipt_trigger_is_handled_by_exactly_its_reactor`: list every class in
  `src/F5Sign/EvidenceAudit/Infrastructure/Messenger/Subscriber/` (glob), classify each as `TRAIL`
  (`OnEnvelopeCompletedHandler`) or `RECEIPT` (the three) — fail naming an unclassified class; the `__invoke`
  parameter types' `EVENT_TYPE`s of the `RECEIPT` ones equal the values of `ReceiptBoundaryFact::cases()`.
- `every_recipient_outcome_is_classified`: exhaustive table over `RecipientOutcome::cases()` → `COMPLETED` ⇒
  `[RECIPIENT_SIGNED, SESSION_COMPLETED]`, `DECLINED` ⇒ `[SESSION_DECLINED]`, `PENDING`, `RELEASED`, `DELEGATED`,
  `SKIPPED` ⇒ `[]`; fail naming an unclassified case; the union equals `ReceiptBoundaryFact` values.
- `every_session_status_is_classified`: over `SessionStatus::cases()`, a status reaches a trigger iff
  `closedByTheRecipientsOwnAct()` (`REVOKED` with no act ⇒ none: AC-14h).

## Prose to write
Each reactor: "Issues the receipt of the recipient whose act this fact ends: translates `<Event>` into `IssueReceipt`
(ADR-0086 §2.2). The command is a function of the fact — its envelope, its recipient, its type and its subject, a
fixed issuer and its own instant — so a redelivery dispatches an equal command and the issuance finds the receipt
issued." Plus, per reactor: `OnRecipientSigned` "Under `SEQUENTIAL_PADES` this, not the commit, is the fact that makes
a signer's or an approver's receipt true: the signature is in the PDF." `OnSessionCompleted` "Under `SINGLE_SEAL`
only: the fact carries no mode, so the use case decides, and under `SEQUENTIAL_PADES` it issues nothing."
`OnSessionDeclined` "The decline's last fact, the only one with the reason; when the decline voided the envelope,
the close is already below it." All three: "Other contexts handle the same delivery. When the issuance throws,
Messenger retries the whole delivery and their handlers run again; each converges (ADR-0072 §2.10 states the same for
the trail)."
