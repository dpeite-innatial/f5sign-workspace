## Goal
`GET /api/v1/envelopes/{id}/recipients/{recipientId}/receipt` (`MACHINE_KEY`) serves the sealed receipt with a
strong `ETag`, 304, 404 when none exists for the tenant, 409 `RECEIPT_UNAVAILABLE` for missing bytes or a
substitution (the second logged at error).

## Why
"`GET /api/v1/envelopes/{id}/recipients/{recipientId}/receipt`, `MACHINE_KEY`, for the integrator." (§3 #5);
ADR-0086 §2.6: "404 when no receipt exists for the tenant, whatever the reason; 409 `RECEIPT_UNAVAILABLE` for missing
bytes or a detected substitution, the second logged at error."

## Prose to write
- Controller docblock: "`GET /api/v1/envelopes/{id}/recipients/{recipientId}/receipt` — downloads the sealed receipt
  of one recipient's act, for the current tenant (ADR-0086 §2.6). UI translation only. Any API key of the tenant may
  download any recipient's receipt of any envelope of the tenant, as for the audit trail. A sealed receipt is never
  rewritten, so it is served with a strong `ETag` (the SHA-256 of the bytes), 304 on `If-None-Match`, `private`. The
  name is `justificante-<envelope id>-<recipient id>.pdf`, two canonical UUIDs, so it needs none of the documents'
  filename strip; a name that ever carries free text must copy it. A detected substitution is logged here, at error,
  before the edge answers 409."
- `#[OA\Get]`: `summary: 'Download the sealed receipt of one recipient\'s act'`, `tags: ['Audit trail']`,
  `security: [['machineApiKey' => []]]`, parameters: the `DeclaredSubjectHeader` ref plus `id` and `recipientId`
  (path, uuid). Responses:
  - 200: "The recipient's sealed receipt: a PDF/A-3a document with its data attached as JSON, sealed by the platform.
    It states that recipient's own act and the sending organisation's name, and nothing about the other participants
    or the person who sent the envelope. It never changes once issued."
  - 304: "Not modified (matched If-None-Match)."; 400 / 401 / 500: the shared refs.
  - 404: "No receipt for this recipient under your tenant. One answer for an unknown envelope or recipient, another
    tenant's, a recipient who has not acted, a role that gets no receipt (only a signer, an approver or a decliner
    gets one), and a receipt not issued yet. A receipt is issued shortly after the recipient's act is recorded: under
    `SEQUENTIAL_PADES` once their signature is in the PDF, under `SINGLE_SEAL` at their commit, and at a decline in
    either mode. So for a recipient who acted, a later request can succeed."
  - 409: "`RECEIPT_UNAVAILABLE`: the receipt is recorded, but its PDF cannot be read. A later request gives the same
    answer unless the platform restores the PDF, which it cannot do for a receipt that was destroyed."
  (keep the `// @infection-ignore-all` lines the sibling carries above each description)
