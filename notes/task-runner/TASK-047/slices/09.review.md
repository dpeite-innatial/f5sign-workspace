## Finding (rule 2, always reported)
`src/F5Sign/EvidenceAudit/Application/Subscriber/OnSessionCompleted.php` docblock: "When the issuance throws, Messenger retries the whole delivery and their handlers run again; each converges". For SESSION_COMPLETED this Y is false in the tree: Signature Execution's `OnSessionCompleted` -> `SignForRecipientUseCase` does not converge for an approver under SEQUENTIAL_PADES; it appends a second `RECIPIENT_SIGNED` with the same execution id (docs/BACKLOG.md BL-463, ADR-0086 §3.3). And the receipt can throw there under SEQUENTIAL_PADES: `IssueReceiptUseCase` runs the registry check and `getEnvelopeContext()` (both may throw) before `ReceiptEligibility::issuesOn()` returns. That is the plan's risk, and it is written down only in `var/task-runner/TASK-047/plan.md` (not in the tree) and as a redelivery in general in ADR-0086 §3.3, which does not say a receipt refusal is one source of it. A reader of this reactor is told the opposite.
Fix: in `OnSessionCompleted`'s docblock, replace "each converges (ADR-0072 §2.10 states the same for the trail)." with wording naming the exception, e.g.: "Envelope's completion converges. Signature Execution's does not for an approver under `SEQUENTIAL_PADES`: it appends a second `RECIPIENT_SIGNED` under the same execution id ([BL-463](../../../../../docs/BACKLOG.md)). That moves neither this receipt's boundary nor the approver's, since the settled read takes the first row of a type and subject and the receipt's id is derived (ADR-0086 §3.3). Under `SEQUENTIAL_PADES` this receipt issues nothing, but its registry check and envelope read come before that answer, so their refusal still redelivers the fact." Leave the other two reactors as they are: their sibling handlers (Envelope, Session on RecipientSigned; Envelope on SessionDeclined) converge.
