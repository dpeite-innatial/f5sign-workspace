# Slice 02 — The receipt is a model of its own, with a closed published JSON

| Tier | critical |
| Covers | AC-5 (closed key set: the tenant's name in, no sender key), AC-6e (chain over the prefix only), AC-15 |
| Depends on | none |

## Goal
`EvidenceAudit/Domain/Receipt` holds the receipt's `final readonly` model and `ReceiptJson`
(`f5sign.receipt/1`) whose key set is closed and pinned by a test; `Domain/ValueObject/ReceiptBoundaryFact`
names the three trigger facts. Shared value types are reused from `Domain/Trail`.

## Why
"A model of its own in Evidence & Audit … **No other participant's data.**" and "The receipt's JSON has a
closed key set; other parties appear neither in it nor on the page." (TASK-047 §3 #3, §4 #3; ADR-0086 §2.3,
§7.2: "a separate model with a closed key set fails loudly instead.")

## Shape (decided here; `Option`/`Statement` are the trail's)
- `Receipt`: `envelope`, `recipient`, `act`, `documents` (list), `boundary`, `chain` (`Trail\Chain`), `limitations`
  (`list<ReceiptLimitationCode>`), `generation` (`Trail\Generation`). `of(envelope, recipient, act, documents,
  boundary, list<Fact> $facts, limitations, generation)` folds `Chain::over($envelope->envelopeId, $held)` where
  `$held` = facts with `position <= $boundary->position`; the facts are not kept.
- `ReceiptEnvelope(envelopeId string, title Statement<string>, tenantLabel Statement<string>, signingMode string,
  closedBefore Option<ReceiptFact>)`. No sender field of any kind (owner, 2026-10-23: the sending organisation is
  stated, the sender person is not).
- `ReceiptRecipient(recipientId string, step int, role string, authentication Option<Authentication>, personal ReceiptPersonal)`.
- `ReceiptPersonal(name Statement<string>, ip, userAgent, acceptLanguage: Option<Statement<string>>, asserted Option<AssertedIdentity>, reported Option<BrowserReport>)`.
- `ReceiptAct(kind ActKind, fact Option<ReceiptFact>, reason Option<string>, reservation Option<Reservation>)`.
- `ReceiptFact(eventType string, position int, at DateTimeImmutable)`; `ReceiptBoundary(eventType, position, subjectId, at)`.
- `ReceiptDocument(documentId string, name Statement<string>, original Statement<Digest>, revision Option<Statement<ReceiptRevision>>)`.
- `ReceiptRevision(artifactSeq int, before Digest, after Digest, bytes CheckOutcome, certificate Statement<Certificate>)`.
- JSON (snake_case, model order): `schema`, `envelope{envelope_id,title,tenant_label,signing_mode,closed_before}`,
  `recipient{recipient_id,step,role,authentication,personal{name,ip,user_agent,accept_language,asserted,reported}}`,
  `act{kind,fact{event_type,position,at},reason,reservation}`, `documents[{document_id,name,original,revision{artifact_seq,before,after,bytes,certificate}}]`,
  `boundary{event_type,position,subject_id,at}`, `chain{algorithm,root,leaves}`, `limitations[{code}]`,
  `generation{at,app_version,typst_version,template_hash}`. Absent options are `null`; shared types use the
  `TrailJson` encoders.

## Prose to write
- `Receipt`: "The receipt of one recipient's act: what the sealed document states, and nothing more
  (ADR-0086 §2.3). It names one recipient and the sending organisation, and no other participant: not the
  person who sent the envelope. Its chain is the `sha256-chain/1` fold of
  the envelope's facts up to and including the act's fact, so it equals the link the envelope's audit trail
  reaches at that position. Like the trail it records and never refuses."
- `ReceiptEnvelope::$closedBefore`: "The first close the log records at or before the act — `ENVELOPE_VOIDED`,
  `ENVELOPE_CLOSED_BY_SENDER` or `ENVELOPE_COMPLETED` — or empty. An agreement's own close records no fact
  (ADR-0073 §2.2), so empty means the log records no close, not that the envelope was open."
- `ReceiptEnvelope::$tenantLabel`: "The sending organisation, named by the label it was provisioned under, as
  the audit trail names it: not its legal name, which no context holds yet. The organisation is not a participant
  and the recipient already saw it in the invitation and the ceremony, so the receipt states it. The person who
  sent the envelope is another party: the receipt states neither their reference, name nor contact."
- `ReceiptJson`: "The published JSON form of a receipt, attached to its PDF. This class is the format's
  specification, under `Receipt::SCHEMA`. Its key set is closed and pinned by `ReceiptJsonTest`: a key added
  here is a published change. Shared values are encoded by `TrailJson`, so both documents spell them alike."
- `ReceiptBoundaryFact`: "The fact that ends a recipient's act and triggers their receipt (ADR-0086 §2.2), as
  the event log names its type. Spelled here because the Domain may not import the event classes; a test keeps
  each value equal to its event's `EVENT_TYPE`."
- Each `TrailJson` method made public: "Shared with `ReceiptJson`: one encoding for a value both documents state."

## Claims you may NOT copy
- `AuditTrail`'s "One value serves three uses … anything not in it cannot appear on the page" applies, but its
  "facts may run past the terminal fact … activity view" does not: the receipt keeps no facts.
