# Slice 13 — A recipient downloads their own receipt with their session credential

| Tier | critical (security route, writes `#[OA\*]`; review on opus) |
| Covers | AC-8a–AC-8d, V-1 |
| Depends on | slice 03, slice 07, slice 11 |

## Goal
`GET /api/v1/signing/session/receipt` (`SESSION_CREDENTIAL`, controller in `EvidenceAudit/UI/Http`) serves the
credential's own recipient's receipt; 409 `RECEIPT_NOT_READY` when their session is `COMPLETED` or `DECLINED` and no
receipt is issued yet; 404 otherwise — including a retired credential and a recipient the closed envelope withholds.

## Why
"The session route answers 409 `RECEIPT_NOT_READY` between the act and the issuance, so the frontend can wait for it
… "Has acted" is the **session's own state**, `COMPLETED` or `DECLINED`, read through Session's contract, not
Envelope's record of the act" (§4 #5). ADR-0060 §2.3 (a retired credential is refused on every credential route);
ADR-0073 §2.15 (a closed envelope withholds every read from a recipient who neither signed nor is informational).

## Prose to write
- Controller: "`GET /api/v1/signing/session/receipt` — the session credential's own recipient downloads the receipt of
  their act (ADR-0086 §2.6). The recipient is the credential's; the route takes no recipient id. Between the act and
  the issuance it answers 409 `RECEIPT_NOT_READY`, decided by the session's own state, because under
  `SEQUENTIAL_PADES` Envelope records the act only on the receipt's own trigger. A retired credential (ADR-0060 §2.3)
  and a recipient the closed envelope withholds (ADR-0073 §2.15) get the same 404 as no act."
- `#[OA\Get]`: `summary: 'Download the receipt of your own act'`, `tags: ['Signing']`, `security:
  [['sessionCredential' => []]]`, no parameters. Responses: 200 "The sealed receipt of your own act: a PDF/A-3a
  document with its data attached as JSON. It names the sending organisation, states nothing about the other
  participants or the person who sent the envelope, and never changes once issued."; 304; 400 / 401 shared refs; 404 "No receipt for you here: you have not signed, approved or declined, your
  credential was retired by the sender, or the envelope has closed and no longer serves you (a recipient who declined
  or was released). Ask `GET /api/v1/signing/session` to tell these apart."; 409 "`RECEIPT_NOT_READY`: you acted and
  your receipt is not issued yet. Under `SEQUENTIAL_PADES` it is issued once your signature is in the PDF; ask again
  after a short wait. It lifts by itself." and "`RECEIPT_UNAVAILABLE`: the receipt is recorded, but its PDF cannot be
  read."; 500.
