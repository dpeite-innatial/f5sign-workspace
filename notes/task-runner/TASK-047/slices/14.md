# Slice 14 — Real flows issue each acting recipient's receipt, and both routes serve it

| Tier | critical |
| Covers | S-1a, S-1b, S-1c, S-2, S-3, AC-1 (acceptance), AC-3, AC-4a (acceptance) |
| Depends on | slice 09, slice 11, slice 13 |

## Goal
Acceptance tests drive envelopes through the platform's own routes and the relay, with the live stack (Postgres,
MinIO, eu-dss, Typst), and download each receipt; the receipt's attached JSON states what the slices promised.

## Why
"A `SEQUENTIAL_PADES` envelope with two signers and an approver, end to end, each receipt downloaded through the
machine route"; "A `SINGLE_SEAL` signer downloads their receipt through the session route right after their commit,
once it is issued"; "A recipient declines; the integrator downloads the decliner's receipt" (§5 S-1…S-3).

## Files

## Scenarios (decided here)
- **S-1** (`a_sequential_envelope_gives_each_acting_recipient_their_own_receipt`): a `SEQUENTIAL_PADES` envelope,
  step 1 signer A, step 2 signer B, step 3 approver C, one document; author it through the bus as
  `SequentialPadesLoopTest` / `IssueAuditTrailPhaseBoundaryTest::author()` do when the seeder cannot declare the
  roles. A signs (session + `POST /signing/commit` with a signature image), pump; B signs, pump; C approves, pump.
  Download each receipt through the machine route. **S-1a** A's JSON: `boundary.event_type` `RECIPIENT_SIGNED`, `act.kind` `SIGNED`, its document's
  `revision.value.artifact_seq` is A's artifact (not B's), `bytes` `HOLDS`, certificate stated. **S-1b** B's names
  B's own revision (a higher `artifact_seq`). **S-1c** C's: `APPROVED`, every `revision` null,
  `APPROVAL_SIGNS_NO_DOCUMENT` listed. None of the three JSONs contains another recipient's name or id.
- **S-2** (`a_single_seal_signer_downloads_their_receipt_with_their_session`): a `SINGLE_SEAL` seed, one signer;
  commit through the route; before the pump `GET /signing/session/receipt` with the session bearer → 409
  `RECEIPT_NOT_READY`; pump; → 200, the JSON carries `NO_PER_RECIPIENT_SIGNATURE`.
- **S-3** (`the_integrator_downloads_a_decliners_receipt`): seed with two steps, step 1 recipient declines through
  `POST /signing/decline` with a reason, pump; machine route → 200, `act.kind` `DECLINED`, `act.reason` the reason,
  `envelope.closed_before.event_type` `ENVELOPE_VOIDED`, decline IP/UA stated.
- **AC-1 (never before)**: in S-1, after A's commit and before the pump the machine route answers 404 for A; after
  the pump A's registry row and JSON name `RECIPIENT_SIGNED` as the boundary, never `SESSION_COMPLETED`.
