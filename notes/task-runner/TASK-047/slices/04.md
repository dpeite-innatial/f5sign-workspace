# Slice 04 — A receipt's sources are gathered up to the recipient's act, and only theirs

| Tier | critical |
| Covers | AC-6d, AC-6c (evidence source), AC-5 (own evidence only; the tenant's name, no sender source), AC-11 (read-back kept as its `Err`) |
| Depends on | slice 01, slice 02 |

## Goal
`ReceiptSourcesGatherer::gather()` reads the envelope's stream up to the act (type + subject), the recipient's
assigned documents' chains, the revision their own execution appended (bytes, prefix digests, read-back) and
their own act evidence, and the sending organisation's name, and returns `ReceiptSources` or a `ReceiptSourcesRefusal`.

## Why
"per assigned document, its original digest and, under `SEQUENTIAL_PADES`, the revision their own execution
appended, picked from the artifact chain by their execution id (never the chain's tip) and read back from the
bytes up to that revision (`PrefixDigests`, `SignatureReadBack`)"; "(`ActEvidenceReader` … answers every
recipient of the envelope, so the receipt keeps only its own recipient's entries before assembling)" (§3 #3).

## DTOs and the rule (decided here)
- `ReceiptSources(ActStream $act, SigningMode $signingMode, Option<string> $tenantLabel, RecipientView $recipient,
  list<ReceiptDocumentSource> $documents, Option<ActEvidenceView> $evidence, Generation $generation)` — no
  envelope-wide view: other recipients cannot reach the assembler. No sender source: the log names the sender only
  by an actor reference, and the gatherer holds no port that reads a user (the two IdentityAccess query ports are
  `TenantNames` and `TenantSmsNotifications`; inject only the first).
- `ReceiptDocumentSource(string $documentId, string $name, bool $signs, Option<DocumentArtifact> $original,
  Option<RevisionSource> $revision)`; `RevisionSource(DocumentArtifact $before, DocumentArtifact $artifact,
  Option<PrefixDigests> $digests, Option<Result<list<ObservedSignature>, ReadBackFailure>> $readBack)`.
- Steps: (1) `readSettledToSubject(envelopeId, [fact->value], subjectId)`; `StreamNotSettled` → `ActNotSettledYet`,
  `NoBoundaryInStream` → `ActNotRecorded`; `ActStream::of(...)->expect(...)`. (2) `chains->forEnvelope()`; `Err` →
  `ReceiptEnvelopeUnreadable`. (3) For each of `$recipient->documentAssignments`, in order: the chain is that
  document's artifacts without `EXTENSION`, in port order; `original` = first; name = the envelope document's
  `displayName`, else the id. (4) Only when `fact === RECIPIENT_SIGNED` and the assignment `signs`: the revision
  is the artifact at index ≥ 1 with `producedBy->kind === SIGN` and `producedBy->executionId` equal
  (case-insensitive) to `subjectId`; `before` is the one before it. Read **that artifact's** custody bytes
  (`CustodyNotFoundException|CustodyBytesUnavailableException` → no bytes; any other exception propagates),
  `PrefixDigests::of($bytes, [$before->sizeBytes])`, `readBack->signaturesIn($bytes, '<documentId>.pdf')`; release
  the bytes before the next document. (5) `actEvidence->forEnvelope()[recipientId]` → `Option`. (5b) `tenantNames->of($envelope->tenantId)` → `$tenantLabel`:
  the tenant Envelope reads the envelope under (the view passed in), never the stream rows' tenant, as
  `AuditTrailSources` asks it; asked once. (6) `Generation`
  from the clock read last, `$appVersion` and the renderer identity.

## Prose to write
- Gatherer: "Gathers the sources of one recipient's receipt through the ports of the contexts that hold them
  (ADR-0086 §2.3). The stream ends at the recipient's own act: its type and its subject. It keeps only this
  recipient's row, documents and evidence, so the assembler cannot state another participant. The sending organisation's name is read as the audit trail
  reads it, for the tenant Envelope reads the envelope under. The revision is
  the artifact this recipient's execution produced, never the chain's tip, which may already hold a later
  signer's revision; its bytes are that artifact's own. One document's bytes are held at a time. A read-back
  failure is kept as its `Err`, so the issuance can tell a signing engine that did not answer."
- `ActStream`: "A settled stream that ends at one recipient's act: a fact of the trigger's type whose subject
  is the trigger's subject (ADR-0086 §2.1)."
- Each refusal: one sentence saying what it means and that the outlook is the issuance's (`ActNotSettledYet`:
  "Another transaction was still writing the envelope's stream. A later call can succeed.").

## Claims you may NOT copy
- `AuditTrailSources`' "The bytes read are those of the final artifact of each document" — here they are the
  recipient's revision's.
