# Slice 12 — A receipt is kept as long as its envelope, once its envelope has ended

| Tier | critical |
| Covers | AC-12a, AC-12b |
| Depends on | slice 07 |

## Goal
`app:evidence-audit:conserve-trails` also conserves issued receipts: for each receipt not yet conserved, it reads
whether `ENVELOPE_COMPLETED` is on the envelope's stream (`EventStreamReader::readSettled` +
`EndedStream::TERMINAL_TYPES`/`EndedStream::of`); only then it raises the receipt's custody to the envelope's
ceiling and records the conservation. A cross-tenant funnel names the tenants that hold such receipts.

## Why
"**The custody stays transient while the envelope is open** and is conserved only once the envelope has ended,
which is `ENVELOPE_COMPLETED` on its stream … `ConservationCeilings::of()` answers for any sent envelope,
provisional or final, so it cannot say this; the job reads the fact through … `EndedStream`, `TERMINAL_TYPES`,
reused, not rebuilt." (§3 #4; ADR-0086 §2.5, §7.4)

## Prose to write
- Command docblock: add a section "**A receipt is kept to the same ceiling, and only once its envelope has ended**
  (ADR-0086 §2.5). A receipt is issued while its envelope may still be open, when the ceiling is still the
  provisional one resolved at the send (ADR-0073 §2.16), and this job raises a custody once. So for a receipt it
  first reads whether `ENVELOPE_COMPLETED` is on the envelope's stream, through the settled read and `EndedStream`, as
  the trail's issuance does; `ConservationCeilings::of()` answers for any sent envelope and cannot say this. A receipt
  of an envelope not ended is left transient and counted as waiting; a transient custody that nobody deleted has no
  end of its own, so waiting loses nothing." Keep the trail sections; fix the first line to name both.
- Migration docblock: "Lets the custodian keep each issued receipt as long as its envelope: the funnel that names the
  tenants holding a receipt not yet conserved (ADR-0086 §2.5). Shaped as `Version20260928090940`, whose docblock
  explains the policy scope, the sentinel pin and the grants; they hold here for `issued_receipt`."
- Read repository: as `TenantsWithUnconservedTrails`, for receipts.
