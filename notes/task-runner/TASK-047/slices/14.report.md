## Concerns
- **Sabotage 4 cannot fire on the slice's roster.** A's receipt is gathered in the pump right after A's signature. B cannot act until A's step completes, so when A's receipt is gathered the chain holds only A's `SIGN` artifact, and "last SIGN" and "A's execution" are the same artifact.
- **I measured a roster that does fire it, with a scratch test that I have since deleted.** I put a second signer beside A on step 1, both committed before one pump, and read each receipt's `artifact_seq`:
  - sabotaged: `seqs A=1 B=2 receiptA=2 receiptB=2`, so A's receipt names B's artifact;
  - clean: `receiptA=1 receiptB=2`.
- **What I did not do:** I left S-1 as the slice decided it (steps 1/2/3), because the roster is a scenario decision and the slice made it. If you want this guard pinned at acceptance, one option is to add a co-signer on A's step to S-1, or to add a fourth test with that roster.
